# 0.0.3

## Added

- Choose a theme in Settings, from Daylight, Neutral, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, Ember, and Cappuccino, and set the contrast and motion, or have each follow your system.
- Manage every workspace in Settings: create, edit, and delete them, and search them by name, with accents ignored and the closest matches first.
- Remove a workspace from Recent in the switcher; it comes back when you open it again.
- The start screen lists your five most recent workspaces, and the title bar stays at the top with no workspace open.

## Changed

- The desktop app draws its own title bar, with Minimize, Maximize, and Close. On Windows, resting the pointer on Maximize shows Snap Layouts.
- The workspace switcher's search shows the best matches first, and Settings lists eight workspaces before See all.
- In the browser, a reload keeps Settings open on the page you were on.
- The web app's desktop download shows the version it downloads, as `v0.0.3`.

## Fixed

- On desktop, opening a folder no longer fails with "Failed to open the selected folder." A folder Vista 0.0.2 remembered may ask to be picked again once.
- A workspace action no longer finishes after a newer one and undoes it, such as a slow folder open replacing the workspace you switched to since. ([#53](https://github.com/gustavohrm/vista/issues/53))
- In the browser, a workspace from a folder picked without file access keeps its tabs when you reopen it in the same session.
- While the browser blocks storage, the Light or Dark scheme you pick stays picked.
- Text fields and switches draw in the theme's outline colour, so they are easier to see.
- Tooltips open on the side where they fit and stay beside their button as the layout changes.
- The workspace switcher stays inside the window as it narrows, and `Tab` moves focus on from it.
